How it works

The host's browser runs the match and shares a 4-letter code. Two friends enter the code and pick a side. Three players are required: one Swarm, two Colonies.

Swarm holds the central plateau: build Extractors on the mineral veins, hatch mobile Spawners on creep (they breed units for free and boost your income), and destroy both Command Hubs.

Colony players each defend a corner base: train Troopers, keep Generators and Batteries up (lasers need energy), expand to geysers, research at a Tech Lab, and kill the Hive.

Every 4 minutes a Surge gives the Swarm more income and each Colony two free Troopers, so nobody can turtle forever. If a Colony player leaves, their ally inherits the base.
